## 1. W22 — a fill's tone against what the picture saw

**Found:** on the Town street (2026-10-30) the road showed a darker trapezoid, the part the set picture saw, against the filled cobbles.

**Since then:** D172 (2026-10-31) refills a ground seen at a slant whole, from above. On the walkthrough's street the road, both
pavements and both facades are now 100 % filled (`fill_mask` under D170 / D172), so the trapezoid cannot recur inside the road. A seam is
left only on a face that is filled in part: the street's far end (17 % filled) and the pier's backdrops (13–14 %).

**E55** (`engine/spikes/e55_seam.py`, CPU only, read-only on the walkthrough's project, journal 2026-11-01 05:37). For each face filled in
part, the tone step at the seam is ΔE between the seen pixels within 12 px of the fill and the filled pixels within 12 px of the seen.
The match maps the filled region's Lab means and spreads, measured on its own band, onto the seen band's.

| Face | Filled | Step before | Step after |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Town street · end | 17 % | 15.1 | 0.06 |
| Harbour pier · backdrop_1 | 13 % | 16.2 | 0.9 |
| Harbour pier · backdrop_11 | 14 % | 23.8 | 1.2 |

- **What the pictures show:** on the pier's backdrop the paler filled sky takes the seen part's teal, and the seam all but goes.
- **The one bad match:** on the street's end the seen band is mostly the letterbox bar (W65), so the fill went darker. That picture would
  now be refused (W65).

**Proposal (D179):** a face filled in part (2–98 %) has its fill matched to its seen part across the seam, as above, when its Fill lands
(CPU, in `fill_landed`). A face filled whole is left as it is.

**Not covered:**

- the tone between faces, such as a refilled road beside a refilled pavement. Each fill is its own picture; matching them to each other is
  a further step if it shows.

## 2. W60 — a part of a set that moves

**Found:** on Departure the ferry is painted into the pier's baked backdrop. Nothing lets it sail.

**What exists:**

- D123 (SC3) does this for a picture set: a thing standing in the place is picked on the picture. The plate is cleaned under it with a
  Fill, and the thing becomes a model.
- A built set (D143) has no such step.
- D173 / D174 play a loop on a face, and D110 places Cast props as 3D models.

**Options:**

- **A · a cut-out card (proposed):**
  - The thing is picked on the set picture, by words ("the ferry") or a click, and SAM 3 cuts it.
  - Its region of the face is filled, so the backdrop shows the water where it stood.
  - It becomes an image plane with its alpha, standing where it was, that Clay can key like any object.
  - Cheap: one matte, one Fill.
  - Flat, which is fine for a thing far off, such as a ferry across the harbour.
- **B · a 3D model:** the cut-out made into a model (D109's image → 3D), as a Cast prop.
  - It turns and has depth, so it suits a thing near the camera.
  - It is heavier, and an image → 3D model of a whole ferry is uncertain: a spike first.
- **C · a card that plays a clip:** the cut-out moving on its own (Wan from the cut-out, as D173's loop), on a card.
  - For water or a flag; a ferry sailing away is keys, not a clip.

**Proposal:** A, with B later for a near thing.
