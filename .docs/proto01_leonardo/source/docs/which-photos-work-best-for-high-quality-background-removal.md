# Which photos work best for high-quality background removal?

remove.bg works best with images that clearly feature a subject in the foreground. This includes most photos of:

* People
* Products
* Animals
* Cars
* Other clearly defined objects

We aim to provide high-quality cutouts across a wide range of subjects and settings. If you're unsure whether your image will work or if you're experiencing issues, feel free to contact us at [support@leonardo.ai](); we're happy to help!

## General recommendations

* **Contrast**: Images taken under good lighting conditions and with a high contrast between foreground and background give better results.<br />

  ![](https://files.readme.io/b9050fd1feaa48f3d65a307c880a6c90cd7a722cef90da80bb074cc62dc488f8-screenshot-help-remove-bg-2021-04-29-14_26_52.png)

* **Alignment**: Make sure to upload images with the correct orientation. Results are best if they match gravity (i.e. the ground is at the bottom of the image).<br />

  ![](https://files.readme.io/f8dedd675d372a1a5833563399f5e115a89f8c57f8f136475c0394026dfa5793-screenshot-help-remove-bg-2021-04-29-15_16_28.png)

* **Prefer plain backgrounds**: Blurry and single-color backgrounds are easier to remove than sharp backgrounds with many details.<br />

  ![](https://files.readme.io/0f338927769ec8efd485d7741e3089e6eaf48e7f3cf5e0dd697087065caed4ef-screenshot-help-remove-bg-2021-04-29-15_17_44.png)

* **Sharp foreground**: If the foreground is blurry it might get removed. If only the edges are blurry, they will still be blurry in the cutout (which may or may not be an issue).<br />

  ![](https://files.readme.io/635b8e5b26d525cfec46dcb227fb19c4640010d1c40454dafb5f98a9f0845ff0-screenshot-help-remove-bg-2021-04-29-15_18_47.png)

<br />

## Recommendations for photos of people

* **Number of people**: Images with one person or with a few people currently perform better than photos of large groups of people.<br />

  ![](https://files.readme.io/93214fd9ac75dc6076a005133094d8a5b26efaa77654186dedbb174c1a3d0136-screenshot-help-remove-bg-2021-04-29-15_39_54.png)

* **Objects**: Small objects held in hands or worn on the body are usually supported (e.g. a basketball), whereas large objects standing out are problematic.<br />

  ![](https://files.readme.io/6d4b5a2254f97cef1fd3c5b5ca0bcf6d0f5204d54bc67dcd5b29dbb86abc1893-screenshot-help-remove-bg-2021-04-29-15_41_34.png)

<br />

## Recommendations for product photos and other objects

* **Whole object**: The whole product should be visible in the image, i.e. it must not be cut off.<br />

  ![](https://files.readme.io/c7921ce4e738ef2664c18ebec3450d8095cef5301ce1f01b672ddd53d6bff360-screenshot-help-remove-bg-2021-04-29-15_43_09.png)

* **Object in focus**: Take products out of their packaging and place them in front of a plain background (photo studio, wall, floor).<br />

  ![](https://files.readme.io/9caa38e297323053ad0327968ff6350ad0e84abe396a0b4b29e4d3abcaf84ce7-screenshot-help-remove-bg-2021-04-29-15_44_16.png)

* **Shadows and reflections**: Try to avoid hard shadows since they can be mistaken to be part of the foreground. Avoid reflections (especially in the background).<br />

  ![](https://files.readme.io/719b5fea61b3d12c537b8b1986d5473c8420d7374731533dbb5dcf1b37b881d4-shadowsreflections_pexels-jess-bailey-designs-1251083.jpg)

* **No decorations**: Do not place decorative objects in the photo. Do not add logos, stamps or clip-arts to the photo before uploading it.<br />

  ![](https://files.readme.io/63c8ce4771facdbc2ca22db38c041b848921762d9fc307e021e4342f5d5977b9-nodecorations_pexels-sunsetoned-5913179-654.jpg)

<br />

## Recommendations for car photos

* **Avoid extreme angles**: Take the photo in a natural perspective, roughly at eye level.<br />

  ![](https://files.readme.io/06ac30a0d1c5af5cc143cb9e655495916ad466c8dff56247c60fd5d156b50992-screenshot-help-remove-bg-2021-04-29-15_49_17.png)

* **Uncovered**: The car should not be obscured by anything in front of it.<br />

  ![](https://files.readme.io/101adf797fdafa2b8b9f97b2a4439c4dd1188fd8a0ed564a6ba605de77d0d004-screenshot-help-remove-bg-2021-04-29-15_50_22.png)

* **One at a time**: For best results, only include one car in each photo. Only the largest visible car will be cut out, everything else is considered background.<br />

  ![](https://files.readme.io/f0000643f2531af268bf9f54f91ec6e9078219f76f99a97db8a99f6ba94de201-screenshot-help-remove-bg-2021-04-29-15_51_28.png)
