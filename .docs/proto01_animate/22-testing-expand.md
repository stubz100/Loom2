# 22 · Trying Expand from Image (for the author)

About 3 minutes. D115: a character's coverage is expanded from the Image suite — Generate as always, or Expand with the poses picked as
icons. The old *Expand…* in Cast always ran one fixed plan; now it jumps here.

## 1. From Cast (≈ 1 min)

- Story ▸ **Cast**, a character with a hero image: **Expand in Image…** (the panel's button, or the right-click menu).
- Image opens with the character as the prompt's subject, **Expand** on under the prompt, and the **Poses** section (the rail's last tab)
  showing Full coverage's tiles lit.
- **Check:** does the grid read at a glance — rows the shot sizes, columns the angles, the head's turn in each icon?

## 2. Pick the poses (≈ 1 min)

- **clear**, then click a few tiles; the chips add or drop expressions for every lit tile (a lit tile shows a count when it has more
  than one). The presets above the grid are quick picks. The line under the grid says how many images and where they land.
- The foot reads *Expand N ▶* (N = the cells × the batch count in Size & count). Over 8 images it asks first, with the minutes.
- **Check:** is "tiles × expressions" the right way to pick, or would you rather pick each cell's expression on the tile?

## 3. Expand (≈ 1 min, GPU)

- **Expand N ▶**. One job per cell; the prompt's style, light, mood, references and the sheet stay, the pose and action are replaced by
  the cell's. The images land as the character's **candidates** in Cast with their pose recorded: keep ✓ / reject ✕ as before, the
  coverage grid fills.
- **Check:** does the first candidate's prompt (the Inspector) read as expected — the sheet, then the pose sentence?
- **Please note** whether a toast "Queued N images for M poses" appears after the click: the e2e saw the jobs but never caught the toast.
