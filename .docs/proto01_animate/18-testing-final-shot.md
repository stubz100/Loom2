# 18 · Looking at the final shot (for the author)

About 10 minutes. The first shot made all the way through loom2's own path: a finished Stage scene, split into layers, each layer made,
then put back together. The files are local, under `engine/spikes/out/ps5/` (not in git).

## What was made

E23's shot on a fresh project:

- **The scene:** Mara and Ines as Cast characters, bound to figures. Mara walks her path; Ines sits on a stool under the window light.
- **Length:** 5 seconds.
- **The steps, all from the final's panel in Animate:**
  - **Make the final shot:** Stage splits the scene into its layers (26 s): the plate, Mara, Ines, the stool, the shadows.
  - **The plate:** a picture of the empty room (38 s), projected along the camera path.
  - **Each character:** performed by Wan Animate from her reference, which stands where her figure starts, in two joined batches
    (≈ 5–6 min each). Then matted, so only she remains (30–50 s).
  - **Assemble the shot (28 s):** the room under Stage's shadows, the stool, then each character set on her shadow (her contact offset).
    Converted from 16 to 24 fps.

## In the app

Animate ▸ Clay ▸ Shot ▸ **Final shot**:

- *Make the final shot…*;
- then *Make the plate*, *Perform* on each character, *Assemble the shot* and *Play ▶*;
- a right-click on a layer to re-take it with a new seed.

Corrections by hand (moving a character, softening a shadow) come with EffectCraft, after spike E27 (D112).
