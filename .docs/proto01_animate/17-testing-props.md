# 17 · Trying the props and the 3D scene (for the author)

About 15 minutes in the app, plus one model job of about 5 minutes. Use a scratch project, not a real one.

## 1. Make a prop (Story · Props)

1. **Story ▸ Props** (the box icon on the rail, Alt+7). Click **New prop…** and name it, for example "Chest".
2. Give it a look in the sheet: "a small red-painted wooden chest with iron corners".
3. Give it an image. Either click **Make an animation reference** (the prop alone, a three-quarter view), or use **Make a prop…** on
   a Catalogue image (the image becomes its hero).
4. In the **3D model** section, click **Make the 3D model…**. The dialog says the cost; queue it.
5. When it lands, the model turns on the page. Drag it to turn it by hand.

- **Check:** does the model look like the prop, and does it stand upright?
- **Check:** Story ▸ Cast no longer lists the prop.

## 2. Build a scene (Animate ▸ Clay)

1. Make a new stage and add the **room** set (now just its floor and two walls).
2. Under **Props**, click your prop. It lands at the viewport's centre as its 3D model.
3. Add a figure or two, and move them with the gizmo.
4. Under **Lights**, click **Add light**. In the Inspector, try the looks (**Window**, **Sun**, **Lamp**), the intensity, colour and
   softness, and **aim at** an object. The dashed line shows the aim.
5. With nothing selected, the stage's Inspector has the **ambient** slider.
6. Look through the camera (the camera button), key a small camera move with **Key camera** at two frames, and play it.

- **Check:** does the scene read as one coherent 3D space, with the props textured and their shadows on the floor?
- **Check:** are the light controls what you need, or is something missing (more lights, a spotlight, keyed light changes)?
